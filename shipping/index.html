Shipping policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Where we ship

We ship to the countries listed at checkout. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your country.

Production time

Each item is printed after you order it. Production usually takes 2 to 5 business days.

Delivery time

After printing, delivery usually takes about one to three weeks depending on your location. These are estimates, not guarantees, and busy periods and customs can add time.

Tracking

You will get an email with tracking details once your parcel ships. You can also check your order page using the link you were sent after checkout.

Split shipments

Items in one order may be printed in different locations and arrive in separate parcels.

Customs and import taxes

Orders shipped outside South Africa may be charged import duties or taxes by your country. These are not included in our prices and are your responsibility.

Problems with delivery

See our refund and returns policy or email .
