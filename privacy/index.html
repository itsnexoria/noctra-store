Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, delivery address, phone number and what you bought.
  • Your email address if you join our mailing list.
  • Basic technical information, such as your browser, needed to keep the site working and secure.

Why we use it

To take payment, make and deliver your order, send order and shipping updates, answer your questions, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. We only send marketing emails to people who joined our list, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack processes your payment.
  • Printify and its print and shipping partners receive your name, address, email and phone number so they can make and deliver your order, which may be in another country.
  • Supabase and Cloudflare host our database and website.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. Because some of these providers operate outside South Africa, your information may be transferred and stored internationally.

Cookies and local storage

We store your shopping bag and recent order references in your browser so the store works. We do not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil your order and meet tax and legal requirements. Mailing list emails are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting . If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Security

We use secure connections and restrict access to customer data, but no system is completely risk free.
