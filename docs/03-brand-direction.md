# Stage 3 (foundation only) — Brand direction

Built around the supplied Noctra wordmark: high-contrast serif, a sharp swash on the N, a four-point star in the "o".

- **Idea**: an independent night label — quiet, precise, a little ceremonial. Voice: short sentences, plain facts, no hype, no fake urgency.
- **Palette**: Ink `#121212`, Bone `#EFEAE0`, Midnight `#17202B`, Brass `#A8895A` (on Ink) / Deep Brass `#7A5F33` (on Bone), Dust `#5F5C55`. No purple, no gradients, not red/black.
- **Type**: Instrument Serif (display) + Instrument Sans (text), self-hosted via `@fontsource` (no external font requests, no tracking).
- **Spacing**: 4 px base scale in `src/styles/tokens.css`; fluid type steps.
- **Layout rules**: asymmetric 12-column compositions, wordmark allowed to bleed off-grid, ruled "ledger" lists instead of card grids, generous whitespace, one idea per screen on mobile.
- **Motion**: one slow star fade-in on the hero; disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Logo files**: `public/brand/noctra-logo-ink.png`, `noctra-logo-bone.png` (transparent, cropped), `noctra-logo-source.png` (your original). Replace with vector (SVG) when available.
- **Imagery**: no stock or placeholder photos presented as product shots. Mockups are labelled "mockup" until real samples are photographed. Artwork is original (scripted star charts from public astronomical data; no third-party marks).

Full guide (components, image specs, tone examples) lands with Stage 3 proper, after the niche is validated.
