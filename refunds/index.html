Refund and returns policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Every Noctra piece is printed especially for you, so we handle returns a little differently from a regular shop. Nothing here affects your statutory rights under the law.

Damaged, faulty or misprinted items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or with a printing error, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We will reprint and resend it, or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Wrong size or change of mind

Because items are made to order, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for the wrong size, colour, or a change of mind. Please check the size guide before you buy.

Cancelling an order

Orders go into production quickly. If you contact us straight away we will try to cancel before printing starts, but we cannot cancel once production has begun.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your tracking shows no movement for 14 days, or the parcel is marked delivered but you did not receive it, contact us within 60 days of ordering and we will investigate with the carrier, then reship or refund.

Wrong address

Please double check your address at checkout. If a parcel is returned because the address was wrong or incomplete, we may need to charge for reshipping.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Paystack and usually take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

How to reach us

Email with your order number (it starts with NX).
